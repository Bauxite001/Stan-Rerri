## DESIGNER NOTES

- **Replace mosaic cells** with real Instagram photos — family moments, travel shots, celebration images. The grid proportions are ready.
- The **orange from the logo is the entire palette** — do not introduce any secondary accent colours
- Every section breathes — **96px vertical padding** on desktop, 64px on tablet, 48px mobile
- **Mobile:** All 2-col layouts collapse to single column · About monogram panel hides · Mosaic becomes 2 columns · Event tag pills hide on mobile
- The site should feel like a **beautifully kept family photo album** — warm, personal, never corporate
- Avoid cold tones, hard shadows, or geometric rigidity anywhere on the page
